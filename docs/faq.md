# FAQ | AnySearch Open Source Bounty Program

> [English](faq.md) | [中文](faq.zh.md)

## Who can participate?

Any developer or developer team can participate, except AnySearch employees and related parties. Participants must comply with the target project's license and rules.

## Can I recommend my own project?

Yes. AnySearch will carefully evaluate self-recommended projects, but they are not eligible for rewards in the current program. Qualifying projects may be included in the target project list for future programs.

## What projects are eligible?

For the current program, bounty-eligible projects are limited to those included in the official project list.

## Can multiple developers claim the same project?

Only **one active Claim ID** is allowed per repository. Other developers enter a queue based on registration order and may take over when the current claim expires.

## Can I submit a PR before registering?

No. You must register and receive a valid **Claim ID** before submitting your PR.

## How long is my Claim ID valid?

You must submit a valid PR within **7 calendar days** after receiving your Claim ID. Otherwise, the claim expires.

## What counts as a qualifying integration?

The integration must provide a **genuinely usable AnySearch capability** within the target project and be successfully merged into its official repository.

## Which integration methods can I use?

You may use a built-in API, MCP, Skill, or other integration method, as permitted by the target project.

## How is the bounty determined?

The specific bounty amount for each target project is determined and published by AnySearch before the program launch in the official project list.

## When will I get paid?

After your PR is successfully merged, passes the review, and your payment information is confirmed, payment will normally be initiated within **14 business days**.


If you have any other questions, please contact mkt@anysearch.com
